# Kit 1 · Your week

Once steps 1 and 2 are done, this is the whole week. Move the days to fit yours; keep the order.

## Monday · Source and idea (about 35 minutes)

- Record your source, or pick one post you already have (step 3).
- Get its text and check it is complete.
- Paste Instruction 1. Pick the core idea (step 4).

## Monday or Tuesday · The set (about 20 minutes)

- Paste Instructions 2 and 3, and 4 for a video source.
- Paste Instruction 5 and fix what it flags.
- Put every piece on your review list (step 5).

## Tuesday · Review and schedule (about 30 minutes)

- Keep, edit or drop each piece.
- Schedule what you keep, Wednesday to next Tuesday (step 6).

## Every day · Answer (about 10 minutes)

- Answer comments and messages.
- Each time someone asks about your service, write one line on the enquiry list: the platform, the piece, their question.

## Friday · Count and keep what works (about 20 minutes)

- Write each piece's comments and enquiries on the posting log.
- Fill in the scorecard from your platforms' own numbers.
- Run the seven Friday checks.
- Paste Instruction 6 with this week's Sheet E and the last three weeks of Sheet F. Pick next week's source from its three ideas (step 7).

**The week adds up to about 1 hour 45 minutes, plus your daily answers.**

---

## Keep three spares

Each week, keep up to three good pieces you did not schedule, for each platform. In a week you cannot record, schedule the spares and keep the rhythm.

## A voice note counts as a source

Had an idea in the car? Record a voice note. If it holds one clear idea, it can be next Monday's source. If the idea is good but the take is not, use the note as your outline and record it properly on Monday.

## When to change the plan

- **A platform brought no enquiries for three weeks running:** keep posting there only if it still brings comments or messages you value. Otherwise drop it and put the time into the platform that brings enquiries.
- **The review takes more than 30 minutes:** cut the pieces a week on Sheet B. A smaller set you review well beats a big set you skim.
- **You skipped a week:** start again on Monday from step 3. Steps 1 and 2 stay done.
