# Kit 1 · The six sheets

Keep them in a notes app, a document or a spreadsheet. In a spreadsheet, each sheet is a tab and each line below is a column.

Write UNKNOWN where you do not know yet. Never fill a box with a guess.

---

Fill in once (step 1). Update it when your offer changes.

- **The problem you solve for them**, in their words:
- **What you sell**, in one line, with its price if you show prices:
- **How people buy or book** (a call, a form, a link, a message):
- **What people ask you most** (three questions):
- **Words you use** (five or more, the way you say them):
- **Never list**: words you never use:
- **Three posts of yours you like**, pasted in full:
- **What you can show as proof today** (a result, a before and after, a client's words, with where it is kept), or UNKNOWN:

- **Source name** (for example S-2026-11-09):
- **Kind:** recording or post
- **Where it is kept:**
- **Text checked complete** (yes, with the date):
- **Core idea picked:**
- **The 4 angles:**

---

## Sheet D · The review list

One line a piece (steps 5 and 6).

- **Piece number**
- **Platform**
- **Angle**
- **Source name**
- **Opening line picked**
- **Status:** keep, edit or drop
- **Reviewed on** (date)
- **Scheduled for** (day and time)

---

## Sheet E · The posting log

One line a scheduled piece (step 6), checked again on Friday (step 7).

- **Piece number** (from Sheet D)
- **Platform**
- **Opening line** (the one you picked on Sheet D)
- **Scheduled for**
- **Went out on** (from the platform's own post date)
- **Link to the post**
- **Comments** (on Friday, from the post itself)
- **Enquiries it brought** (on Friday, its lines on Sheet F's enquiry list)

---
