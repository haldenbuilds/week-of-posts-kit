# Kit 1 · The Friday check

Seven questions. Answer each with **pass**, **fail**, or **could not run** and why. Write the answers on Sheet F.

**The one rule:** never write "pass" for a check you did not run. A check you could not run is "could not run", with the reason. A week with a "could not run" is not a passed week.

The week worked when all seven pass.

---

## 1 · Five platforms or fewer, all ones you already used

- **Look at:** Sheet B.
- **Pass:** no more than five platforms, and you posted on each one before this kit.
- **If it fails:** cut the list back to the platforms you already use.

## 2 · One complete source behind the whole week

- **Look at:** Sheet C and Sheet D.
- **Pass:** every piece on Sheet D names the same source, and Sheet C says its text was checked complete.
- **If it fails:** pieces with another source or no source come off this week's list.

## 3 · The set you agreed reached your review list

- **Look at:** Sheet B and Sheet D.
- **Pass:** for each platform, Sheet D holds the number of pieces Sheet B sets.
- **If it fails:** run Instruction 2 again for the missing platform.

## 4 · Nothing went out before you reviewed it

- **Look at:** Sheet D (reviewed on) and Sheet E (went out on).
- **Pass:** every piece that went out was reviewed before it went out.
- **If it fails:** take the piece down if it needs it, and review before you schedule from now on.

## 5 · Reviewed pieces went out on schedule

- **Look at:** Sheet E. Read "went out on" from each platform itself, not from your scheduler.
- **Pass:** every scheduled piece went out on its day, on every platform you picked.
- **If it fails:** check that platform's scheduler and its connection to your account.

## 6 · Your word reached you, and got an answer

- **Do:** from a second account, or ask a friend, comment or message your word on one of this week's posts.
- **Pass:** you saw it within a day and it got an answer.
- **If it fails:** turn on notifications for comments and messages on that platform, and test again.
- **No word yet?** Then send a test message asking about your service. Same pass line.

## 7 · Nothing went out that you did not agree

- **Look at:** each platform's own list of this week's posts, against Sheet E.
- **Pass:** every post that went out this week is on Sheet E.
- **If it fails:** find what posted it (an old schedule, a tool, a team member) and stop it.
