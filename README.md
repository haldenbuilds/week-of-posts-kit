# Kit 1 · The Week of Posts Kit

**What it gets you: one week of posts for the platforms you already use, made from one recording or one post you already have.** You review every piece before it goes out. Each Friday you also count the enquiries your posts brought.

**Your time:** under 2 hours a week once it runs, plus about 10 minutes a day to answer comments and messages. The first week takes about 3 hours, because you set it up.

## What you need

- A business that sells a service: coaching, consulting or a service firm.
- One complete recording (a video, a talk, a voice note) or one post you already have.
- The social accounts you already post on. This kit adds no new platform.
- An assistant you already pay for, for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Any one that reads text you paste in will do.
- A place for the kit's sheets: a notes app, a document or a spreadsheet.

## What is in this kit

1. **1-STEPS.md**: the seven steps, in order. Each says what to do, what to paste, how you know it worked, and how long it takes.
2. **2-INSTRUCTIONS.md**: the instructions you paste into your assistant, word for word.
3. **3-SHEETS.md**: the six sheets you fill in.
4. **4-CHECKS.md**: the Friday check. Seven questions that tell you the week worked.
5. **5-WEEKLY-RHYTHM.md**: your week, day by day, once it runs.

**Start here:** open 1-STEPS.md and do step 1.

## What this kit does not do

- It does not pick what you talk about. You pick the idea.
- It posts nothing by itself. You approve and schedule every piece.
- It promises no reach, followers or sales. It gives you a steady week of posts and a count of what they bring.
- It never logs in to your accounts. Everything stays where you already work.
