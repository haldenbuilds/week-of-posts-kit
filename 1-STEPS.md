# Kit 1 · The seven steps

Steps 1 and 2 you do once. Steps 3 to 7 you do every week.

A step is done only when its check passes. If a check fails, fix it before the next step.

---

## Step 1 · Your business on one page

**Once. About 30 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Fill in **Sheet A** (3-SHEETS.md). If you already have an offer sheet, copy from it.
2. Open your assistant. Paste **Instruction 0** (2-INSTRUCTIONS.md), with Sheet A at the end.
3. If your assistant lets you save standing instructions (a project, a custom assistant, a Gem), save Instruction 0 there. Then you do not paste it each week.

**Check**
- Ask your assistant: "In two sentences, who do I serve and what do I sell?"
- It passes if the answer matches Sheet A and adds nothing you did not write.
- If it adds a claim, a number or a promise, correct it and ask again.

---

## Step 2 · Your platforms and your weekly set

**Once. About 15 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Fill in **Sheet B**: every platform you post on today.
2. Pick up to five of them. Only platforms you already use.
3. For each one, write how many posts a week you will review: 1 to 4, because step 5 writes one piece for each of your four angles. 3 or 4 a platform is plenty.
4. For each one, write your call to action. For example "Comment GUIDE and I will send it" or "Message me the word GUIDE".

**Check**
- No more than five platforms.
- Every platform on the list is one you already posted on before this kit.
- Each platform has a number of posts from 1 to 4 and a call to action.

---

## Step 3 · This week's source

**Every week. 10 to 40 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Pick one source: a recording of 5 to 20 minutes on one topic, or one post you already have.
2. No recording yet? Record yourself answering the question your buyers ask most. Your phone is enough.
3. Get the words as text: your recording app's transcript, your video tool's captions file, or upload the audio to your assistant if it accepts audio.
4. Write it on **Sheet C**.

**Check**
- The text runs from your first word to your last.
- Read the first and the last minute of the text against the recording.
- A text that stops early or skips a part fails. Get it again.

---

## Step 4 · The core idea and its angles

**Every week. About 10 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Paste **Instruction 1** with the text of your source.
2. You get three core ideas, each with four angles.
3. Pick one core idea. Write it on Sheet C.

**Check**
- You can say the core idea in one sentence, in your own words.
- Each angle is a different side of the same problem, not the same point said four ways.
- Each angle quotes the lines of your source it came from.

---

## Step 5 · The content set

**Every week. About 20 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Paste **Instruction 2**. You get one piece for each angle on each platform, up to the number you set on Sheet B. Each comes with five opening lines to pick from.
2. Paste **Instruction 3**. You get one long version and one qualifying question.
3. Video source? Paste **Instruction 4**. You get the clip list with start and end times. Cut the clips in the editor you already use.
4. Paste **Instruction 5**, the self-check. Fix or delete everything it flags.
5. Put every piece on **Sheet D**, your review list.

**Check**
- Sheet D holds the number of pieces you set for each platform on Sheet B.
- Every piece names its platform, its angle and its source.
- The self-check lists no claim, number or name marked NOT IN SOURCE, and no open [CHECK].

---

## Step 6 · Review and schedule

**Every week. About 30 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Read every piece on Sheet D. Mark each one keep, edit or drop.
2. Schedule the pieces you keep, in each platform's own scheduler or the scheduler you already use.
3. Write each one on **Sheet E** with its day and time.

**Check**
- Every scheduled piece shows "reviewed" on Sheet D, with a date before its post time.
- Nothing is scheduled that is not on Sheet D.

---

## Step 7 · Answer, count and keep what works

**Every day about 10 minutes. Fridays about 20 minutes.**

**Do**
1. Every day, answer comments and messages. Each time someone asks about your service, write one line on the enquiry list on **Sheet F**: the platform, the piece it came from, and their question in their words.
2. On Friday, write each piece's comments and enquiries on **Sheet E**, and fill in the rest of Sheet F, from your platforms' own numbers.
3. Run the Friday check (4-CHECKS.md).
4. Paste **Instruction 6** with this week's Sheet E and the last three weeks of Sheet F. Use its answer to pick next week's source.

**Check**
- Every number on Sheet E and Sheet F says where you read it, or "could not read" and why.
- Every line on the enquiry list has its platform and their question.
- All seven Friday checks are answered: pass, fail, or "could not run" and why.
